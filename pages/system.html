Restart, power down, or restore your BlueRetro adapter.

Your saved settings are only erased by Factory reset. The adapter will disconnect from this page after any action.

Disconnect controllers from BlueRetro first, then select the adapter in your browser.

Restart adapterReboots BlueRetro without changing saved settings.
Enter deep sleepPuts the adapter into its lowest-power state until it is powered again.
Restore factory settingsErases the adapter configuration and returns it to its defaults.
