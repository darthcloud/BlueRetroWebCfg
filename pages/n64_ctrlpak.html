Back up, restore, or erase the virtual N64 Controller Pak banks stored by BlueRetro.

Direction matters: Back up downloads saves from BlueRetro. Restore uploads a local file and replaces the selected bank.

Disconnect controllers from BlueRetro first, then select the adapter in your browser.

Back up this bankDownloads the selected bank to your PC as an .mpk file.
Restore this bankReplaces the selected bank with an .mpk file from your PC.
Erase this bankFormats the selected bank as an empty Controller Pak.

You can inspect an .mpk backup with N64 Mempak Tool.
