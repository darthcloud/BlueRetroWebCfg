Back up or restore the virtual Dreamcast VMU stored by BlueRetro.

Direction matters: Back up downloads the VMU from BlueRetro. Restore uploads a local file and replaces its current contents.

Disconnect controllers from BlueRetro first, then select the adapter in your browser.

Back up the VMUDownloads the current VMU to your PC as a .bin file.
Restore the VMUReplaces the current VMU with a .bin file from your PC.
